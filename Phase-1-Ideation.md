# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
PocketSmart AI: Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant

## Problem Statement
Many people find it difficult to manage their income, expenses, budgets, and savings effectively. They may not clearly understand their spending patterns or know how to plan their money efficiently.

## Proposed Solution
PocketSmart AI is an AI-powered budget and recommendation assistant that helps users record their income and expenses, understand their spending, create budgets, set savings goals, and receive personalized recommendations.

## Objectives
- Help users manage their personal budget.
- Track income and expenses.
- Provide spending summaries.
- Help users set savings goals.
- Generate personalized financial recommendations using AI.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

## Target Users
- Students
- College students
- Working professionals
- Individuals who want to manage their personal finances

## Key Features
- Income and expense management
- Budget planning
- Expense categorization
- Savings goal tracking
- Spending analysis
- AI-powered recommendations
- User-friendly dashboard

## Expected Outcome
The project aims to provide users with a simple smart budgeting assistant that helps them understand their spending and make better-informed financial planning decisions.
